About India

India is a land of rich heritage, offering diverse cultures and breathtaking landscapes for every traveler.

Top Travel Destinations

1. Agra

State: Uttar Pradesh

Description: Famous for the majestic Taj Mahal and Mughal architecture.

Fact: The Taj Mahal changes color depending on the time of day.

2. Jaipur

State: Rajasthan

Description: Known as the Pink City, famous for palaces and forts.

Fact: It is home to the world's largest stone sundial at Jantar Mantar.

3. Goa

State: Goa

Description: Popular for sandy beaches, vibrant nightlife, and seafood.

Fact: Goa was ruled by the Portuguese for over 450 years.

4. Manali

State: Himachal Pradesh

Description: A beautiful hill station nestled in the Himalayas.

Fact: It is named after the legendary sage Manu.

5. Kerala

State: Kerala

Description: Known as God's Own Country, famous for backwaters and tea gardens.

Fact: It is considered the birthplace of Ayurveda medicine.

Travel Tips

  1. Always keep local currency cash with you.
  2. Book train and flight tickets well in advance.
  3. Respect local customs and dress codes at temples.
  4. Keep emergency helpline numbers saved.
  5. Drink bottled or filtered water while traveling.
